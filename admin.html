Step 1: Connect your website folder

Choose the portfolio-site folder (the one containing index.html). Your new work and settings are saved straight into it. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

This page needs Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer. Your current browser can't save into folders.

Where messages from "Let's work together" go

Your website is a static site, so messages need an inbox service. The easiest free option is Formspree (formspree.io): it stores every submission in a dashboard and emails you.

  1. Create a free account at formspree.io and click New form.
  2. Copy the form link (it looks like https://formspree.io/f/abcdwxyz).
  3. Paste it below, add your email and the dashboard link, then Save.

After saving, upload your folder to your host again so the live site gets the new settings.
